DATA INTEGRITY · SOURCE METHODOLOGY

Traceable by design.
Useful by evidence.

Every procurement notice in TenderAtlas is sourced from a published government or public-sector register. We make the coverage and limitations clear, so you can research opportunities without confusing a published notice with an open, eligible bid.

01 / EU

Tenders Electronic Daily

Searches the EU Publications Office TED Search API v3 for recent competitive notices. The public API requires no key. Selected notice types are retrieved; not every TED announcement or national-only procurement is included.

Searches Greece's public notice registry. Provides a direct official PDF attachment where the record contains an ADAM reference. The publisher refreshes open data approximately every 24 hours. Notices can be modified, cancelled, or expired.

03 / UK

Find a Tender · OCDS

Uses the UK release-package API. To control latency, the service inspects a bounded number of recent release pages (up to two without a query; up to three for keyword search), not the full historical UK catalogue. This is not exhaustive keyword search.

When a server-side SAM.gov public API key is configured, US government notices can be queried. The key never belongs in the browser or repository. Until configured, US-only search reports that coverage is not enabled.

What a result means — and what it does not

A source record contains what the publisher released at the time of retrieval. Dates, values, CPV codes, buyer names, and procedural categories are shown only when present. Blank fields are left unverified. A listed deadline is not proof that a tender is still open, and a relevance score is not an eligibility judgment or an award probability.

How source errors are handled

  • Sources are requested independently. If one fails, the interface identifies the missing feed rather than silently filling the gap with generated records.
  • Counts represent records in the fetched result pages, not all opportunities in a country or market.
  • Repeated official IDs are deduplicated within the response. Cross-country notices may still refer to related procurement procedures.
  • Recent UK release scanning is bounded. It cannot guarantee that all keyword matches in the requested publication window have been found.
  • The underlying publisher may revise or remove records. Use the official link and downloaded documents immediately before making a participation decision.

How matching and AI are used

When the account and optional Workers AI services are configured, company profiles may provide keyword/CPV/country relevance and semantic similarity. These are decision-support signals, not legal determinations. They do not infer missing criteria, certifications, or bid-winning likelihood.

Important: This product is a research and discovery interface, not an official tendering portal. TenderAtlas does not submit bids or replace the latest notice, amendments, procurement documents, or the contracting authority's instructions.
